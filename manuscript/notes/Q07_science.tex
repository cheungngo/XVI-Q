\section{Q07: $\kappa^3$ three-point rows --- h h c, evanescent counterterms, pole-level $\sigma_0$ closure}
\subsection*{Scope}
One loop, $\kappa^3$, UV-pole level, declared XVI-P prescription (DRED, opaque $d$, MS-bar, Feynman $+i0$). Rows of
$(S,\Gamma_1^{\rm pole})$ with three external legs: $c\phi\phi$ (closed, Q05), $cc\bar c$ and $ccK$ (4D part closed,
Q06), and $hhc$ (this release). Four-point and higher $\kappa^3$ rows and finite parts are not addressed.
\subsection*{Row h h c}
Channels: (A) $\Gamma_{hhh}$ (G01+G02+G08+G14+G15) with one leg replaced by $s_0h(c)$; (B) own $\Gamma_{hh}$ pole
(graviton, ghost and scalar loops) with one leg $s_1h=\tfrac12 L_c h$; (C) $\delta S_{EH,3}/\delta h\cdot\Gamma_{Kc}$;
(D) $\delta S_2/\delta h$ (EH + gauge fixing) $\cdot\Gamma_{Khc}$; (E) $s\bar c=\sigma_F F(h)$:
$F\cdot\Gamma_{\bar c c h}$ (the channel missing from the Q04 ledger, erratum Q06-E2). The tree row
$(S,S)/2|_{hhc}$ closes exactly with $\sigma_F=i$ and requires channel E. The scalar loops G14, G15 and the scalar
part of $\Gamma_{hh}$ were recomputed with the Q05 engine; the matter sector alone closes exactly (incl.\ evanescent
terms). Result: the 4D part of the row vanishes identically with all channels at $+1$ (the unconstrained fit has the
unique solution all-equal), every single-channel deletion and a flip of $\sigma_F$ re-open it; an explicit
evanescent remainder (248 terms, every term containing a $\tilde g$ contraction) is left.
\subsection*{Evanescent counterterms}
(i) The naive p22-type choice (subtract the $\tilde g$ part of every loop input) is \emph{not} $\sigma_0$-consistent:
tree vertices regenerate $\tilde g$ structures from 4D loop parts (22 / 26 terms left on $ccK$ / $cc\bar c$).
(ii) Joint test: $CT=-\Gamma^{\rm pole}+X$ with $X$ a generic local, purely evanescent $\kappa^3$ functional
(monomials with at least one $\tilde g$) for $\Gamma_{Kch}$, $\Gamma_{\bar c ch}$, $\Gamma_{c^*cc}$ and a Bose-symmetric
$\Gamma_{hhh}$; the same $X_{Kch}$, $X_{\bar c ch}$ enter the ghost rows and $hhc$. The linear system over
$\mathbb Q(i)$ (821 unknowns) is solvable; a particular solution ($X_{c^*cc}=0$) is certified and verified by
substitution; 152 parameters stay free (non-unique). Not tested: ghost-equation compatibility of $X$, four-point rows.
\subsection*{Outcome}
$\sigma_0$ closure at $\kappa^3$: CLOSED at pole level on the one-loop three-point rows in the enlarged (evanescent)
counterterm domain. P.6 remains Partial: CT-domain reduction to an invariant operator basis, four-point rows, finite
parts, ghost-equation compatibility and uniqueness of $X$ are open. O.1 (scoped, $\kappa^2$ two-point) unchanged;
bulk symmetry restoration not claimed.
