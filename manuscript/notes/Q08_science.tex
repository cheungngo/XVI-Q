\section{Q08: structure of the $\kappa^3$ evanescent counterterm}
\subsection*{Scope}
Pole level, one loop, $\kappa^3$, three-point rows (c$\phi\phi$, cc$\bar c$, ccK, hhc) as in Q07. Unknowns: the local
evanescent counterterm $X$ of Q07 (821 coordinates: $X_{Kch}$ 134, $X_{\bar c ch}$ 81, $X_{c^*cc}$ 18, Bose-symmetric
$X_{hhh}$ 588).
\subsection*{1. Ghost equation}
The loop poles obey $\Gamma_{\bar c ch}=\rho\,\Gamma_{Kch}[K\to T(\bar c)]$, $T(\bar c)=\mathrm{sym}(k,\bar c)-\tfrac12(k\cdot\bar c)\eta$,
with one constant $\rho=-2$ (graph by graph: G04/G03 and G09/G07). Imposing $X_{\bar c ch}=-2X_{Kch}[K\to T]$ keeps the
system solvable; a particular solution is certified ($A x=b$ verified). Free directions drop from 152 to 104. The
$\eta$-trace is taken at the $d\to4$ residue level (hat trace 4, tilde trace 0).
\subsection*{2--3. Free directions and trivial families}
Kernel $N$ of the rows: dim 152 (104 with the ghost equation). Single-sector blind directions: 69 in $X_{Kch}$ alone,
12 in $X_{c^*cc}$ alone, 26 in $X_{hhh}$ alone ($\sigma_0$-closed evanescent cubic structures, candidates for cubic
parts of invariant evanescent operators), none in $X_{\bar c ch}$ alone. BRST-trivial candidates $\sigma(Y)$, gh $-1$:
$Y=K\cdot F(h,h)$ (field redefinition of $h$), $Y=c^*\cdot G(c,h)$, $Y=\bar c\cdot\Phi(h,h)$ (gauge fermion). The relative
normalisation of the two pieces of each family was fitted (best uniform $\lambda$: $-1$, $i/2$, $-2$); with it at
least 97 directions of $N$ (55 inside the ghost-equation subspace) are trivial, at most 116. At least 36 directions
are not shown trivial. Whether they are physical ambiguities (invariant evanescent operators) is not settled, and no
explicit operator basis was constructed.
\subsection*{4. Consistency with p22}
Open: the cubic completion of the p22 $\kappa^2$ evanescent operators was not built.
\subsection*{Outcome}
P.6 Partial. Q07's pole-level $\sigma_0$ closure on the three-point rows now holds with a ghost-equation compatible
$X$. Not implied: four-point rows, finite parts, uniqueness/physicality of $X$, p22 consistency.
