``$\geq36$ unexplained'' as a count of non-trivial directions; the Q09 counts ``F verified on 139/204'' and ``derived

\subsection*{Blind directions and the restated bound}
42 X coordinates (30 in the $K\!\cdot\!c\!\cdot\!h$ sector, 12 in the $c^*\!cc$ sector) enter no three-point row and
no Bose condition. 24 of these survive the ghost equation. They are free at this order and unconstrained by the
rows. $\dim N-\dim(N\cap\mathrm{span\ of\ all\ Q08\ pieces})=152-116=36$ is exact linear algebra, but the spanning
pieces are not verified $\sigma$-images. So 36 bounds only the directions outside that span. It is neither a lower
bound on physical directions nor an upper bound on trivial ones.
