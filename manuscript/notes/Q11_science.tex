\section{Q11: BRST-trivial directions as $\sigma$-images in the gauge-fixed BV variables}
\subsection*{Construction}
The gauge fixing is the canonical shift by $\Psi=\bar c\cdot F(h)$ with $B$ integrated out. So the antifield of $h$
enters only through $\tilde K=K+\bar c\cdot F'(h)$, and the Q08 ghost equation ($\rho=-2$) is this statement on the
amplitudes. A local ghost-number $-1$ functional must therefore be built from $\tilde K$: $Y_F=\tilde K\cdot F(h,h)$ has
the $\bar c$ partner $\rho\,T(K\cdot F)$, and the Q08 $P$ family is absorbed into it. $Y_G=c^*\cdot G(c,h)$ gives
$X_{Khc}$ ($c^*\to D^T\tilde K$), its $\bar c$ partner, and the $c^*cc$ piece ($h\to s_0h$, antisymmetrised in the
two ghosts). Q08 lacked that last piece. $\sigma=(S_0,\cdot)$ is applied with the same channel operators that build
the rows, with the antibracket sign $-1$ between antifield-type and field-type pieces. Every generator gets one test:
rows, Bose and ghost equation. No per-generator fit is made.
\subsection*{Results}
Q08 $F$ pieces violate only the $h\!\cdot\!h\!\cdot\!c$ row. 12 generators fail through the antifield-type piece alone,
because the $\bar c$ partner and the $E$ channel are missing. With $\tilde K$: 71 of 194 nonzero $F$ images and 8 of 33
$G$ images lie in $N$, all of them also satisfying the ghost equation. The remaining failures are all in the
$h\!\cdot\!h\!\cdot\!c$ row (for $G$, also in the ghost rows). A $(\lambda,\mu)$ scan, used only as a diagnostic, shows
that no single normalisation works for every generator. Failures occur with and without trace monomials, so the
$\eta$-trace hypothesis is not the (sole) cause. Conclusion: on the evanescent sector of the $h\!\cdot\!h\!\cdot\!c$ row,
the row operators do not compose to zero with this $\sigma$. A candidate cause is the 4D-momentum rule
$T(p,\cdot)=0$ for hat components, under which $s_0\hat h=0$. This is OPEN.
